== SCHEME KNOWLEDGE BASE ==
Domain: Indian Government Loan Schemes and Subsidies for MSMEs and Entrepreneurs

--- MUDRA (Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency) ---
Full Name: Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) - MUDRA Loans
Objective: Provide collateral-free loans to non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises.
Eligibility: Any Indian citizen with a business plan for a manufacturing, trading, or service activity. No collateral required.
Maximum Loan Amount: Up to ₹10 Lakh (₹10,00,000)
Interest Rate: 7-12% depending on bank and loan category
Subsidy: None (but interest subvention of 2% available on Shishu loans)
Social Category Preference: Applicable to all; SC/ST/OBC/Minority communities have specific allocation targets under MUDRA.

Three Categories:
1. Shishu: Loans up to ₹50,000 — for businesses in early/startup stage
2. Kishore: Loans from ₹50,001 to ₹5,00,000 — for established businesses needing growth capital
3. Tarun: Loans from ₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000 — for businesses needing higher credit for expansion

Activities Covered: Small manufacturing, shopkeeping, vegetable/fruit vendor, artisans, food service, repair shops, transport operators, etc.
Documentation: KYC documents, business plan, address proof, ID proof, caste certificate if applicable.
Gender: Women entrepreneurs get priority in many banks under MUDRA; some banks offer lower rates.
Application: Through PSU banks, private banks, MFIs, NBFCs. Online via udyamimitra.in.
Best For: Project costs below ₹10 Lakh, new entrepreneurs, rural and semi-urban businesses.

--- PMEGP (Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme) ---
Full Name: Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme
Objective: Generate employment by setting up micro-enterprises through credit-linked subsidy.
Administered By: KVIC (Khadi and Village Industries Commission)
Eligibility: 
- Age: 18 years and above
- Education: Class 8 pass for projects above ₹10 Lakh in manufacturing, ₹5 Lakh in service
- New units only (no existing businesses)
- Income criteria: No income limit for beneficiaries
Maximum Project Cost:
- Manufacturing sector: ₹50 Lakh (₹50,00,000)
- Service sector: ₹20 Lakh (₹20,00,000)
Loan Amount: 90-95% of project cost (balance is beneficiary contribution)
Subsidy (Margin Money Subsidy):
- Urban area + General Category: 15% of project cost
- Rural area + General Category: 25% of project cost
- Urban area + SC/ST/OBC/Women/Minorities/Ex-servicemen/Differently-abled/NER/Hill/Border: 25%
- Rural area + SC/ST/OBC/Women/Minorities/Ex-servicemen/Differently-abled/NER/Hill/Border: 35%
Interest Rate: Commercial bank rates (approximately 8-10%)
Repayment: 3-7 years (after 6-12 months moratorium)
Gender: Women applicants get higher subsidy (25% urban, 35% rural).
Social Category: SC/ST/OBC applicants get higher subsidy same as women.
Application: Through KVIC, State KVIC offices, District Industries Centres (DIC), banks.
Best For: Manufacturing/service startups in villages, SC/ST entrepreneurs, women entrepreneurs.

--- STAND-UP INDIA ---
Full Name: Stand-Up India Scheme
Objective: Promote entrepreneurship among SC/ST and women by facilitating bank loans.
Eligibility:
- SC/ST individuals: Any business sector
- Women entrepreneurs: Any business sector
- Green field projects only (first venture in manufacturing, services, or agri-allied)
- Must be 18 years or above
Maximum Loan Amount: ₹1 Crore (₹1,00,00,000)
Minimum Loan Amount: ₹10 Lakh (₹10,00,000)
Loan Type: Composite loan (term loan + working capital)
Interest Rate: MCLR + 3% (approximately 11-12% as of 2024)
Subsidy: None (but credit guarantee through CGTMSE)
Collateral: May not be required due to CGTMSE coverage
Repayment: Maximum 7 years with up to 18 months moratorium
Social Category: Exclusively for SC/ST and Women entrepreneurs.
Gender: Women from any category are eligible.
Application: Through scheduled commercial banks. Online portal: standupmitra.in.
Best For: SC/ST and women entrepreneurs starting their first business with project cost ₹10 Lakh to ₹1 Crore.

--- CGTMSE (Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises) ---
Full Name: Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises
Objective: Provide collateral-free credit guarantee to MSMEs through member lending institutions.
Eligibility: Existing or new MSMEs (manufacturing and service sector). Not for retail trade.
Maximum Credit Guarantee: Up to ₹5 Crore (₹5,00,00,000)
Guarantee Coverage: 75-85% of credit facility (80% for loans up to ₹5 Lakh to women/SC/ST)
Annual Guarantee Fee: 0.75-1.5% of loan amount
Benefit: Banks lend without collateral as CGTMSE covers their risk.
Social Category: Higher coverage for women and SC/ST borrowers.
Application: Through banks/NBFCs that are CGTMSE member lending institutions.
Best For: MSMEs that lack collateral but have viable business plans; project costs ₹1 Lakh to ₹5 Crore.

--- NSIC (National Small Industries Corporation) Schemes ---
Full Name: National Small Industries Corporation
Relevant Schemes:
1. NSIC Credit Support:
   - Single Point Registration: MSMEs registered with NSIC get advantages in government tenders.
   - Credit facilitation: NSIC helps MSMEs access bank loans through tie-ups with banks.
   
2. Raw Material Assistance:
   - Helps MSMEs procure raw materials at competitive prices.
   - Short-term credit facility for raw material procurement.

3. Marketing Support:
   - MSME-Mart e-commerce portal for marketing MSME products.
   - Government procurement portal GeM (Government e-Marketplace) preference for NSIC-registered units.

Eligibility: Micro and Small enterprises with Udyam Registration.
Best For: Manufacturing MSMEs looking for government contracts and raw material support.

--- KVIC (Khadi and Village Industries Commission) Schemes ---
SFURTI (Scheme of Fund for Regeneration of Traditional Industries):
- Creates clusters of traditional industries (khadi, village industries, coir, handicrafts).
- Funding: ₹2.5 Crore for artisan clusters.

ASPIRE (A Scheme for Promotion of Innovation, Rural Industries and Entrepreneurship):
- Promotes rural entrepreneurship.
- Sets up Livelihood Business Incubators (LBI) in rural areas.
- Best For: Rural artisans, handicraft workers, village industries.

--- NHB (National Horticulture Board) Schemes ---
Relevant for fruit/vegetable processing, floriculture, organic farming businesses.
Subsidy: 20-40% capital subsidy on project cost.
Best For: Agriculture and food processing businesses in horticulture sector.

--- SITRA / Textile Sector Schemes ---
Technology Upgradation Fund Scheme (TUFS):
- For textile and jute industries.
- 15% subsidy on power loom upgradation.
- For weaving and processing units: 10% capital subsidy.
Best For: Textile manufacturing businesses.

Amended TUFS (ATUFS):
- Capital subsidy of 15% for garment and technical textile machinery.
Best For: Garment manufacturers.

--- SOLAR AND GREEN ENERGY SCHEMES ---
PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana: Solar for homes, can also be used for micro-enterprises.
MNRE Schemes for Solar MSMEs: Subsidy for solar energy-based industries.
Best For: Businesses with high power costs wanting to reduce electricity bills.

--- STATE-LEVEL SCHEMES (Examples) ---
Maharashtra: Chief Minister's Employment Generation Programme (CMEGP), similar to PMEGP with state subsidy.
Gujarat: Atmanirbhar Gujarat Sahay Yojana — interest-free loans up to ₹1 Lakh for small businesses.
Tamil Nadu: NEEDS (New Entrepreneur-cum-Enterprise Development Scheme) — 25% subsidy for youth entrepreneurs.
Uttar Pradesh: One District One Product (ODOP) — special support for traditional handicraft MSMEs.
Rajasthan: Rajasthan Investment Promotion Scheme — capital subsidy for manufacturing units.
Punjab: Punjab MSME Loan Scheme — 5% interest subvention for micro enterprises.
Bihar: Mukhyamantri Udyami Yojana — ₹10 Lakh loan at 0% interest for SC/ST entrepreneurs.

--- SCHEME SELECTION LOGIC ---
How to match a business to the right scheme:

By Project Cost:
- Below ₹50,000: MUDRA Shishu
- ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000: MUDRA Kishore
- ₹5,00,000 to ₹10,00,000: MUDRA Tarun
- ₹10,00,000 to ₹1,00,00,000: Stand-Up India (if SC/ST or Women), PMEGP (if manufacturing/service startup)
- Above ₹1,00,00,000: CGTMSE backed commercial loan, PMEGP for manufacturing up to ₹50L, sector-specific PLI

By Social Category:
- SC/ST: Stand-Up India (₹10L-₹1Cr), PMEGP with 35% rural / 25% urban subsidy, Bihar Mukhyamantri Udyami Yojana
- Women: Stand-Up India, PMEGP with enhanced subsidy, state-level women entrepreneurship schemes
- OBC/Minorities: PMEGP with enhanced subsidy rate
- General: MUDRA, PMEGP standard rates, CGTMSE, NSIC

By Sector:
- Manufacturing: PMEGP (best subsidy), CGTMSE, TUFS (textiles), PLI schemes
- Service/Retail: MUDRA, PMEGP (service variant), NSIC marketing support
- Agriculture: NHB schemes, NABARD refinancing, FPO-linked credit
- Handicrafts: SFURTI, ODOP, NSIC marketing
- Food Processing: PMEGP, PMFME (PM Formalization of Micro Food Processing Enterprises)

By Geography:
- Rural: PMEGP rural subsidy (25-35%), MUDRA, ASPIRE, KVIC schemes
- Urban: PMEGP urban subsidy (15-25%), MUDRA, Stand-Up India, CGTMSE

--- PMFME (PM Formalization of Micro Food Processing Enterprises) ---
Objective: Support unorganized food processing micro enterprises.
Subsidy: 35% credit-linked capital subsidy on eligible project cost (maximum ₹10 Lakh per unit)
Target: Existing micro food processing units.
Best For: Home-based food enterprises, small food manufacturers, street food vendors scaling up.

--- SPMCIL / GeM Procurement ---
GeM (Government e-Marketplace): 
- MSMEs registered on GeM get preference in government procurement.
- ₹25,000 crore annual procurement target from MSMEs.
- No bid security for MSMEs below ₹50 Lakh orders.
Registration: Udyam registration required.

--- UDYAM REGISTRATION ---
All scheme applicants must have Udyam Registration (formerly MSME registration).
Classification:
- Micro: Investment up to ₹1 Crore, Turnover up to ₹5 Crore
- Small: Investment up to ₹10 Crore, Turnover up to ₹50 Crore
- Medium: Investment up to ₹50 Crore, Turnover up to ₹250 Crore
Benefits: Priority sector lending, government procurement preference, subsidies, easier compliance.
Registration: Free on udyamregistration.gov.in.
